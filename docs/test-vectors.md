# Golden vectors
- 2500 kcal thiamine model: 1.5–1.7 mg/day.
- Low gastric acid reduces non-heme iron only; heme iron remains separately modeled.
- Low gastric acid reduces food-bound B12 release but not crystalline B12's food-protein release.
- A 0.5 mg actual intake maps below a 1.2 mg RDA marker; UL remains a marker, never a target.
