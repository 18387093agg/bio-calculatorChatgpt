# Calculation model

The canonical pipeline is portion normalization → food/method preparation yield → nutrient retention → chemical form → gross intake → qualified meal interactions → condition-specific effects → bioaccessibility → absorption → systemic pool → conversion → active pool → target comparison. It is orchestrated by `calculateNutrientPipeline`; unsupported stages are `null`, never fabricated zeroes. No selected condition means a healthy baseline; there is no generic pathology multiplier.

Yield and retention remain distinct. Form-aware iron and B12 ranges are marked as modeled estimates with evidence/assumptions; daily zinc plus phytate can use the Miller population saturation model only when explicitly scoped as a day. Condition effects name the mechanism, nutrients, stage, evidence, and uncertainty; qualitative effects never alter a number. The thiamine project optimization model is energy-based (0.60–0.68 mg/1000 kcal), separate from official references and explicitly a model assumption.

SBS is a clinician-documented, anatomy-specific context, not an alias for ileal resection or Crohn's ileal involvement. The resolver distinguishes no-colon jejunostomy, jejunocolonic anatomy without terminal ileum, jejunoileocolonic anatomy with terminal ileum, and documented intestinal failure/nutrition support. It reports digestion, absorption, loss, adaptation, and management mechanisms qualitatively. SBS terminal-ileum and bile-acid mechanisms suppress overlapping ileal-resection/Crohn mechanisms; remaining bowel length, adaptation time, and oral/parenteral prescriptions do not generate coefficients.

CKD distinguishes documented stages 1–2 without dialysis, stages 3–5 without dialysis, hemodialysis, and peritoneal dialysis. Vitamin-D activation is a conversion mechanism; calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, and potassium are renal-handling contexts; iron is systemic/anemia context; and possible dialysis losses are loss context. None changes intestinal absorption or official population references, and no clinical restriction is presented as an RDA.

Chronic liver disease distinguishes stable and decompensated non-cholestatic disease from cholestatic disease. Hepatic conversion/storage/transport and systemic status mechanisms are not intestinal malabsorption. Only documented cholestasis adds a bile-mediated digestion mechanism for A, D, E, and K; no common fraction is applied. Status observations, clinical supplements, and losses do not predict serum values, stores, absorption, or requirements.
